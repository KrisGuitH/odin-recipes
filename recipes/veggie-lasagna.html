Veggie Lasagna

The Recipe

This vegetarian lasagna is a delicious and healthy alternative to traditional lasagna, packed with fresh vegetables and herbs.

The recipe below is easy to follow and meant for four people.

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 2 cups ricotta cheese
  • 1 cup grated parmesan cheese
  • 1 cup chopped spinach
  • 1 cup chopped zucchini
  • 1 cup chopped bell peppers
  • 1 cup chopped mushrooms
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Cook lasagna noodles according to package directions.
  2. In a large skillet, heat olive oil and sauté garlic until fragrant.
  3. Add spinach, zucchini, bell peppers, and mushrooms. Cook until vegetables are tender.
  4. Stir in ricotta cheese and parmesan cheese until well combined.
  5. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
  6. Layer the cooked noodles and vegetable mixture in a baking dish.
  7. Bake for 30 minutes or until bubbly and golden brown.
